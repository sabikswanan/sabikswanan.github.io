About

Data in, decisions out.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at BRAC University in Dhaka. Most of my time goes into applied machine learning: cleaning messy data, comparing models honestly, and explaining what they learned.

My thesis asks a question that matters a lot in Bangladesh: where will a river bank erode next? I'm building a pipeline that reads decades of satellite imagery of the Jamuna, predicts erosion risk, and writes the result up in plain language.

Outside research I like shipping complete things: a MERN travel platform with AI itineraries, a 3D tank game written from scratch in OpenGL, and a six-site enterprise network with automatic failover.
